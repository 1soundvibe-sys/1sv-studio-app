Why Consistency Wins

Catalog Building

Building a Music Catalog That Lasts

A music catalog is one of the most valuable assets an independent artist can own. Catalog depth — the body of work you own and control — determines your long-term income, your leverage in negotiations, your ability to license music, and your credibility with labels, managers, and industry professionals.

Building catalog intentionally means recording with a plan, not just inspiration. It means mixing and mastering every record to a professional standard so it stands the test of time. It means knowing which songs to release, when to release them, and how to build a release that adds to your story rather than just filling a content calendar.

At 1 Soundvibe Studios in Houston, our mixing and mastering services are available both as standalone services and as part of our Artist Residency programs. Every record we help create is built to meet professional release standards.

Our artist development program includes song review, rollout planning, and guidance on building a catalog strategy that actually supports your goals.

Release Preparation

How to Prepare a Music Release the Right Way

Most independent artists release music the wrong way — not because they lack talent, but because no one showed them the process. A release is not just uploading a file to a distribution platform. It is a coordinated event that involves content, strategy, timing, metadata, and promotion working together.

  • 🎚️
    Professional Mix & Master

    Your record needs to compete sonically with everything on playlist. Start with professional mixing and mastering.

  • 📋
    Metadata & Credits

    ISRC codes, proper split sheets, and accurate credits protect your ownership and publishing.

  • 📱
    Content Strategy

    Build a content plan 3–4 weeks before release to drive early streaming momentum and fanbase growth.

  • 🌐
    Distribution Setup

    Choose the right distribution strategy for your goals. Our Label Residency includes dedicated distribution guidance.

  • 🗓️
    Release Timing

    Submit to DSPs at least 3 weeks early for playlist consideration. Fridays are standard release days for a reason.

  • 🔗
    Playlist & Press Outreach

    Independent playlist pitching and press outreach should begin 2–3 weeks before your release date.

Our Pro Artist and Label Residency programs include hands-on rollout planning and release strategy so every release you put out is positioned properly — not just uploaded and hoped for.
